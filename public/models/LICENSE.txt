ExoView anatomical model assets
================================

This directory holds the per-system anatomical GLB assets loaded at runtime by
the 3D viewer:

  muscular.glb
  skeletal.glb
  respiratory.glb
  cardiovascular.glb

These binary assets are NOT included in the repository. They must be produced by
the offline asset pipeline documented in docs/MODEL_MAPPING.md from a
license-cleared source (e.g. Z-Anatomy or BodyParts3D, both CC BY-SA).

Until the GLB files are added here and `MODELS_ENABLED` is set to `true` in
src/viewer/modelManifest.ts, the viewer renders the procedural mannequin
fallback and no assets are fetched.
